Brown is the defining visual identity.


Every object must be constructed using:


* brown ink
* stippling
* pointillist dots
* engraving lines
* cross-hatching
* dense ink clusters
* sparse ink particles


Dark areas should become dense masses of brown dots and engraved lines.


Light areas should gradually dissolve into the parchment background.


Never use smooth digital gradients.


All shading must appear physically printed.

# 6. PARCHMENT BACKGROUND


The background should resemble aged, warm historical paper.

It must NOT look like dirty paper.


It must NOT look damaged.


It must NOT contain writing.


It must NOT contain visible letters.


It must NOT contain borders.
