# Dedicated Shorts artwork

Three portrait illustrations were created with the built-in image-generation
tool, using the existing landscape artwork as references. Landscape originals
are preserved. All portraits have quiet central space for runtime captions;
particle motion is added by the offline loop builder, never baked into AI art.

The black portrait artwork is a procedural 720x1280 render of the existing
black/mist style: `enigmatic_black_shorts_plate.png`. No image model is required.

## religious

Reference: `religious_renaissance_plate.png`.
Saved portrait artwork: `religious_renaissance_shorts_plate.png`.

Exact prompt:

```text
Use case: compositing. Recompose the supplied reference artwork as a dedicated 9:16 portrait YouTube Shorts background, not a cropped widescreen image. Preserve the same uninhabited Catholic Italian Renaissance chapel, old-master oil-painting medium, classical rounded arches, marble, aged gold ornament, burgundy drapery and warm sacred lighting. Vertical layout: cross high in the upper quarter, a low altar in the bottom quarter, balanced columns visible along both edges; a broad, quiet shadowed central wall through the middle 40% for large white centered subtitles. Keep recognizable chapel details above and below the text zone. Elegant, contemplative and professional. No people, faces, hands, statues of people, text, letters, logo, watermark, border or baked floating particles. The particles and captions will be animated and added separately. Output a complete tall 9:16 artwork.
```
